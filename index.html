How to Spot Crypto Giveaway Scams

Offers that feel too generous

Real programs cap rewards at realistic amounts.

Requests for seed phrases or private keys

No legitimate site will ever ask for your seed phrase.

Unknown token approvals

Approvals let an unknown spender move your tokens later. Read every approval carefully.

Look-alike domains

Clone sites resemble the real one by a character or two. Always return via bookmark.
